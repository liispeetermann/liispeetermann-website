2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites store on your computer, smartphone or other device when you visit a website. They allow websites to recognise your device, remember preferences, maintain security and understand how visitors interact with website content.

We may use cookies and similar technologies, including:

  • First-party cookies: Cookies placed directly by our website or its hosting platform.
  • Third-party cookies: Cookies placed by external service providers whose services are integrated into our website.
  • Session cookies: Temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser.
  • Persistent cookies: Cookies that remain on your device for a specified period or until you delete them.
  • Local storage: Browser storage technology that allows websites to retain information on your device without using traditional cookies.
  • Tracking pixels and similar technologies: Small elements that may help measure website visits, interactions and the effectiveness of communications or advertising.
