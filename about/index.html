About

My approach stems from a lifelong curiosity about the relationship between the body, mind, and the way we live.

My interest in health began with Tibetan Medicine. In 2021, recommendations from a Tibetan Medicine doctor made a significant difference to my own health, and I began studying the system in depth. What started as personal exploration became a long-term commitment to understanding the body and the factors that shape our wellbeing.

Before this, I spent 14 years in New York and Los Angeles, building my own branding consultancy within the international design, technology and startup communities. It shaped how I think independently, solve problems and understand people in their environments.

Formal dance training in New York gave me a lasting foundation in movement, alignment and body awareness, which I have since deepened through body conditioning, strength training and tension release.

Today I bring these disciplines together through hands-on supportive therapies and individual online consultations, combining Tibetan Medicine with nervous system regulation, physical conditioning and contemporary science. I believe meaningful change begins with understanding how your constitution, lifestyle, stress and environment shape both physical and emotional wellbeing, rather than applying the same solutions to everyone.

My work is guided by compassion, attentiveness and respect for each person’s experience. My aim is to make a deeper understanding of the body more accessible, so you can take a more informed and conscious part in your own health.
